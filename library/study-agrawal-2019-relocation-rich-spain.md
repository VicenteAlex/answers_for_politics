## Question
Do top earners move between Spanish regions in response to regional differences in top income tax rates?

## Method and data
After 2011 regions set their own top IRPF rates; administrative Social Security records on regional moves of top earners, 2005-2014.

## Findings
- Elasticity of the number of top taxpayers with respect to net-of-tax rates: 0.85.
- The mechanical revenue increase from higher regional top rates is larger than the revenue lost from net out-migration.

## Limitations
Within-country mobility; reported residence may not equal actual residence. DOI not recorded (not verified). Card based on the abstract.

## Relevance
Shows that mobility of top earners across CCAA is real but did not make higher top rates revenue-losing in 2011-2014.
