## Question
How is tax evasion distributed across the wealth distribution, and what does it imply for measured inequality?

## Method and data
Leaked offshore client lists and amnesty participants matched to population-wide administrative wealth data; comparison with stratified random audit results.

## Findings
- Offshore tax evasion is concentrated at the very top: the top 0.01% evade about 25% of their taxes.
- Random audits detect evasion below 5% throughout the distribution, i.e. audits miss sophisticated offshore evasion.
- Top wealth shares rise substantially once hidden wealth is included.

## Limitations
Leaks are not random samples; extrapolation requires assumptions. Data mostly predate automatic exchange of information (CRS).

## Relevance
Explains why wealth-tax and top-income statistics may understate top wealth, and why enforcement (information exchange, specialised units) matters for Spain's IP/ITSGF.
