## Question
How did co-payment changes affect initial medication non-adherence by income?

## Method and data
Segmented logistic regression (ITS), stratified by income; no control group.

## Findings
- Initial non-adherence rose in poor, low/middle-income pensioners and low-income non-pensioners (OR 1.047-1.370).
- When the 1-euro charge was suspended and low-income people got free medicines, initial non-adherence fell (OR 0.676).

## Limitations
ITS without control group; overlapping policies (regional euro-per-prescription, national 2012 reform).

## Relevance
Distributional evidence: co-payment effects concentrate on low-income groups; removing them reverses part of the effect.
