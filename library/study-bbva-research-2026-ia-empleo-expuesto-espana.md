## Question
How much Spanish employment is exposed to generative AI, and has employment in exposed occupations fallen?

## Method and data
Occupations classified into four groups by exposure to generative AI ("capacidad de esta tecnología para realizar o asistir en las tareas"); employment tracked by group since 2022 (2022 = 100). The page does not name the exposure index or the microdata source (likely EPA; not verified).

## Findings
- Medium-high and high exposure occupations: just over 30% of total employment.
- High exposure: over 40% of workers with higher education vs 15% with ESO or less; over 80% of employment in ICT, finance-insurance and real estate; over 40% of employment in Madrid; about a third of Spanish nationals vs just over 20% of foreign workers.
- Since 2022 employment grew in all four groups, especially medium-high and high exposure; the authors say it is too early for causal conclusions but no negative relation is observed.

## Limitations
- Grade D: descriptive; exposure ≠ displacement; method and index not documented on the page; no authors listed; growth figures only in a chart.
- Published by a bank's research service (interested in AI-driven productivity narratives; stated here for transparency, not as a finding).

## Relevance
Most recent Spain-specific descriptive evidence. Consistent with Albanesi et al. (2025) and with Danish nulls; does not examine young entrants, the margin where US data show declines.
