## Question
How does reported wealth respond to wealth tax rates in a decentralized system, and what explains the response?

## Method and data
Panel of Swiss cantonal wealth tax rates and reported wealth; administrative records from two cantons with quasi-random reform timing used to decompose the response.

## Findings
- A 1 pp lower wealth tax rate raises reported wealth by at least 43% after six years.
- About 24% of the effect is mobility across cantons; about 21% is capitalization into housing prices; saving responses are small; the residual is attributed largely to evasion/under-reporting.

## Limitations
No third-party reporting of financial wealth and highly decentralized rates make the response large; decomposition relies on two cantons. Card based on the abstract.

## Relevance
Close analogue to Spain's regional (CCAA) wealth tax, where moving residence within the country is cheap; supports concerns about subnational tax competition.
