## Question
Does the arrival of refugees in a municipality change local voting?

## Method and data
Denmark assigned refugees to municipalities in a quasi-random way (no choice of location), removing the bias from immigrants sorting into particular places. Municipal and parliamentary election results.

## Findings (abstract)
- In most municipalities, a larger refugee allocation increased vote shares of right-leaning, anti-immigration parties.
- In the largest urban municipalities, the effect was neutral or reversed, consistent with an urban-rural divide in attitudes.
- Allocation also affected where anti-immigration parties chose to run candidates and possibly turnout.

## Limitations
Effect sizes not retrieved; Danish context; refugees rather than labour migrants.

## Relevance
Political feedback: immigration inflows themselves shift electoral demand for restrictive parties, especially outside large cities. Relevant to interpreting Vox's growth and the salience of immigration in Spain.
