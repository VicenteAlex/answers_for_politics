## Question
Did the growth of Airbnb raise rents and house prices in Barcelona?

## Method and data
Neighbourhood panel with very detailed rent and price data (transaction and posted). Approaches: fixed effects with heterogeneous trends, shift-share instrument (tourist amenities predict where listings locate; Google searches predict when), event studies, and the Sagrada Familia case.

## Findings
- Average neighbourhood: Airbnb activity increased rents by 1.9%, transaction prices by 4.6% and posted prices by 3.7%.
- Top decile of Airbnb activity: rents up about 7%, transaction prices about 17% and posted prices about 14%.

## Limitations
Period before Barcelona's later tightening of tourist-flat licensing; effects are relative between neighbourhoods and may understate city-wide general-equilibrium effects. Partly funded by the Ajuntament de Barcelona, which regulates tourist flats.

## Relevance
Shows that conversion of residential units to other uses (tourist rentals) raises both rents and sale prices locally. Relevant to Barcelona's plan not to renew 10,101 tourist-flat licences in November 2028 and to the shift of supply toward seasonal rentals after caps.
