## Question
Which occupations and countries are exposed to generative AI, and is the effect more likely automation or augmentation?

## Method and data
GPT-4 scored the exposure of tasks in ISCO-08 occupations; occupations classified by mean and dispersion of task scores into "automation potential" and "augmentation potential"; applied to ILO employment data by country income group.

## Findings
- Main effect likely to augment rather than automate (abstract).
- Clerical work is the most exposed group; greatest impact in high- and upper-middle-income countries.
- ILO news release: 5.5% of total employment in high-income countries potentially exposed to automating effects; about 0.4% in low-income countries; augmentation potential nearly equal across countries; women's share potentially affected by automation more than twice men's.
- No Spain-specific figure verified.

## Limitations
- Grade D: LLM-rated exposure; not an outcome. Numbers verified against the ILO release, not the paper's tables.
- Superseded methodologically by the 2025 refined index (WP 140).

## Relevance
Gender channel (clerical work) relevant to Spain's occupational structure. Not peer-reviewed.
