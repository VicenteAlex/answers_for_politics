## Question
How exposed are occupations worldwide to generative AI, using a refined task-level index?

## Method and data
Representative sample from 29,753 tasks of the Polish occupational classification; survey of 1,640 people, expert Delphi-style discussions and AI model predictions; four increasing exposure gradients mapped to ISCO-08 and ILO employment data.

## Findings (abstract)
- Globally, one in four workers is in an occupation with some generative-AI exposure; most jobs are expected to be transformed rather than made redundant (research brief).
- 3.3% of global employment falls in the highest exposure category (Gradient 4): 4.7% of female vs 2.4% of male employment.
- Overall exposure: 11% of total employment in low-income countries vs 34% in high-income countries.
- In high-income countries, Gradient 4: 9.6% of female vs 3.5% of male employment.
- No Spain/EU-specific figure verified in the abstract.

## Limitations
- Grade D: exposure index; not an outcome. Not peer-reviewed.
- Task content taken from Polish classification; country exposure depends only on occupational mix.

## Relevance
Offers a more conservative highest-exposure share than the IMF note. The gender gap in the highest gradient is relevant to Spain, where clerical employment is female-dominated.
