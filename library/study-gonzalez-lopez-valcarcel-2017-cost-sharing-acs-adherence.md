## Question
Did the 2012 co-payment change reduce adherence to secondary-prevention drugs after acute coronary syndrome?

## Method and data
Cohort with control group (low-income workers whose coinsurance did not change), DiD/ITS-type comparison; adherence from dispensing records.

## Findings
- No change for cheap drugs (antiplatelets, beta-blockers).
- Pensioners: adherence -6.8% (ACEI/ARB) and -8.3% (statins) vs controls (p<0.01).
- Statin non-adherence +7.8% in middle/high-income group. Effects appeared temporary.

## Limitations
One region; dispensing-based adherence; no clinical outcomes.

## Relevance
Supports a moderate, partly transitory adherence loss from the 2012 co-payment among pensioners.
