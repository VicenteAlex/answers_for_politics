## Question
How did RDL 16/2012 change health-care use, satisfaction and self-assessed health of undocumented immigrants?

## Method and data
DiD (likely undocumented vs Spanish nationals), comparison of regions that enforced vs did not enforce, and triple difference. Status proxied by nationality group without dual nationality. Sample ends September 2015.

## Findings
- Significant fall in planned care (GP/specialist) not fully compensated by emergency use.
- Sharp drop in satisfaction with emergency services.
- Larger effects where regions enforced the exclusion.
- Suggestive evidence of worse self-assessed health. Abstract gives no point estimates.

## Limitations
Small immigrant samples in a general survey; self-reported outcomes; status proxy (ITT).

## Relevance
Consistent mechanism for the mortality result of Juanmartí et al.: less planned care, partial substitution by emergencies.
