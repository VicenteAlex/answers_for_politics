## Question
Did removing undocumented immigrants' access to non-emergency public health care in September 2012 increase their mortality?

## Method and data
Treatment intensity = share of each nationality that was undocumented in 2011 (Census vs residence permits); nationalities with ~0% undocumented as controls. DiD and event study on monthly deaths by nationality and cause, INE 2009-2015 (2.77 million deaths). SEs clustered by nationality.

## Findings
- Monthly mortality of undocumented immigrants +0.31 per 100,000, about 82 extra deaths per year (lower bound); +16.7% on average three years after the reform.
- Effect concentrated in amenable (treatable) causes (+34.3%); no effect on external causes (placebo).
- Parallel pre-trends; selective out-migration explains about 3.5% of the effect.

## Limitations
Undocumented status proxied at nationality level (intention-to-treat). Regional enforcement varied (some regions kept partial coverage), which likely biases toward zero. Undocumented population likely undercounted. Single study, not replicated.

## Relevance
Most credible causal evidence on the health cost of RDL 16/2012's coverage restriction. It speaks to the coverage component only, not to co-payments or spending cuts.
