## Question
How do wealthy taxpayers respond to a (often temporary) personal wealth tax where enforcement is weaker?

## Method and data
Administrative wealth-tax microdata 1993-2016 linked to Panama Papers records; bunching at liability notches and difference-in-differences across four reforms that changed rates and duration.

## Findings
- Declared wealth falls immediately when the wealth tax applies.
- Effects persist years after a temporary tax expires (hysteresis).
- Responses are driven by misreporting of items without third-party verification (overstated debts, understated business assets).
- The wealthiest respond by hiding assets in offshore entities.

## Limitations
Weaker administrative capacity than in Europe; magnitudes are context-specific. Card based on the NBER abstract (published version not read).

## Relevance
Relevant to temporary designs (Spain's ITSGF was enacted as temporary) and to the valuation of unlisted business assets and debts in Spain's IP.
