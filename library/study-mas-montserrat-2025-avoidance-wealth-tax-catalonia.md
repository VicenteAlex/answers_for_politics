## Question
How did wealthy taxpayers respond to the 2011 reintroduction of Spain's wealth tax, and through which margins?

## Method and data
Panel of the universe of wealth-tax returns in Catalonia 2011-2015; variation in average tax rates across taxpayers; examines composition of assets (exempt business assets) and income (to use the combined IRPF-IP 60% cap).

## Findings
- A 0.1 pp increase in the average tax rate reduces cumulative taxable wealth by 3.21% over four years (working paper: 3.24%; elasticity 0.64).
- No negative effect on saving.
- Avoidance via exemptions for business-related assets and via the joint liability limit; the liability-cap channel accounts for 92.6% of the revenue effect.
- The four-year (2012-2015) revenue loss equals 2.75 times the 2011 estimated revenue (WP: 2.6).

## Limitations
One region; short window; results reflect Spanish design (business exemption, cap) and may change with design.

## Relevance
Main Spanish evidence that the IP's base erosion runs through legal design features (exemption of family-business shares, the 60% cap) rather than reduced saving; relevant for any reform of IP/ITSGF.
