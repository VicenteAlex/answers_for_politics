## Question
Does gaining legal status change immigrants' criminal behaviour?

## Method and data
Released prisoners from the 2006 Italian collective pardon; nationals of new EU member states (Romania, Bulgaria) gained legal status in January 2007 while comparable candidate-country nationals did not. Difference-in-differences in re-arrest hazards.

## Findings (abstract)
- "Legal status leads to a 50 percent reduction in recidivism."
- Legal status explains roughly one half to two thirds of the observed gap in crime rates between legal and illegal immigrants.

## Limitations
- Population of former inmates, not all immigrants.
- EU citizenship confers more than legal residence (free movement), so the treatment is broader than a typical regularisation.

## Relevance
Central evidence on the mechanism "irregular status -> fewer legal opportunities -> more crime". Relevant to Spanish regularisations (2005, 2026) and to proposals that would increase the stock of irregular residents (abolishing arraigo) or reduce it.
