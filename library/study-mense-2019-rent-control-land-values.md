## Question
Does a rent cap that exempts new construction change the value of buildable land?

## Method and data
Comparison of land values in municipalities with and without the 2015 rent brake, using propensity-score weighting (short P&P paper; full text not opened).

## Findings
A positive effect of second-generation rent control on the value of vacant, buildable land (abstract). Mechanism proposed: controlled rents shift demand into the uncontrolled new-build segment, raising expected rents there and thus land values.

## Limitations
Short conference paper; identification weaker than in the authors' 2023 JUE paper; magnitudes not recorded here.

## Relevance
One of few causal pieces on asset-price effects of rent control. It concerns land for exempt new construction, not the sale price of existing dwellings. It illustrates that the sign of price effects on the uncontrolled segment depends on design (what is exempt).
