## Question
Did deportation-oriented enforcement (Secure Communities) reduce crime?

## Method and data
Difference-in-differences using the staggered county activation; also elasticity of crime with respect to detained immigrants using county-month detainer counts.

## Findings (abstract)
- The programme "has had no observable effect on the overall crime rate", despite about 250,000 detentions.
- Elasticity estimates not retrieved for this card.

## Limitations
US context; short post-period; crime-report data (reporting by victims may itself respond to enforcement).

## Relevance
Evidence that enforcement focused on removing immigrants did not measurably lower crime. Combined with Mastrobuoni & Pinotti (2015) it suggests legal status and labour-market access matter more than removals for crime outcomes.
