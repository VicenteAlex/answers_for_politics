## Question
Does a local public investment programme that temporarily reduces unemployment also reduce crime?

## Method and data
Nationwide local infrastructure investment policy implemented after the 2008-09 recession, studied in Catalonia with monthly data for more than 900 municipalities. Difference-in-differences and IV, exploiting the as-good-as-random approval of local investment plans by the Ministry of Public Administration.

## Findings (abstract)
- The policy gave a short-term boost to local public finances and temporarily reduced local unemployment.
- Crime rates dropped significantly (magnitudes not taken from the abstract).
- The policy did little to offset the recession's long-run effects.

## Limitations
- One region; jobs created were temporary.
- Post-2008 recession policy, outside the 2016-2026 window.

## Relevance
Spanish causal evidence for the labour-market channel: policies that raise legal employment locally reduce crime. Supports using employment effects as the link when assessing SMI or other labour policies.
