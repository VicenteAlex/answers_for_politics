## Question
How was the 2012 exclusion implemented and offset across regions?

## Method and data
Policy and document analysis. Related: Cimas et al. (2016, Health Policy 120:384-395, doi 10.1016/j.healthpol.2016.02.005) map regional differences in coverage after RDL 16/2012.

## Findings
- About 870,000 people estimated excluded (figure close to the government's 873,000 health cards of non-resident foreigners withdrawn, Programa Nacional de Reformas 2013, which counts cards, not people excluded from care).
- Several regions kept partial coverage; the central government challenged them and the Constitutional Court annulled several regional norms.

## Limitations
Descriptive; no counterfactual.

## Relevance
Context for interpreting causal studies: exposure to the exclusion varied by region.
