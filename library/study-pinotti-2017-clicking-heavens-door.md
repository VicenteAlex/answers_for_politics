## Question
Does legal status reduce immigrants' criminal behaviour?

## Method and data
Italian work permits were allocated under fixed annual quotas; applications were filed online on specific "click days" and processed in order of arrival until the quota ran out. Applicants just before and just after the cut-off (minutes apart) are comparable. Application data matched to individual criminal records.

## Findings (abstract)
- Legalization reduces the crime rate of legalized immigrants by 0.6 percentage points on average, from a baseline of 1.1% (about -50%).

## Limitations
- Applications are employer-sponsored; applicants are not representative of all irregular residents.
- Effect on the treated individuals; does not measure aggregate crime or possible pull effects of regularisation on future irregular inflows.

## Relevance
Most credible estimate of the individual-level crime effect of obtaining legal status. Relevant to Spain's 2005 regularisation and the 2026 extraordinary regularisation (RD 316/2026), which also tie status to work or residence.
