## Question
How much did dispensed prescriptions change after pensioners started paying in 2012?

## Method and data
ARIMA forecasts (trained Jan 2003-May 2012) as counterfactual for Jun 2012-Jul 2013, for each region.

## Findings
- Prescriptions fell >10% in 15 of 17 regions, >15% in 12, >20% in Catalonia, Valencia and Galicia.
- Level shift, no trend change.

## Limitations
No control group; simultaneous delisting of 417 medicines and other measures; volume, not health.

## Relevance
Size of the volume response; part of the fall may be reduced waste or stockpiling before July 2012.
