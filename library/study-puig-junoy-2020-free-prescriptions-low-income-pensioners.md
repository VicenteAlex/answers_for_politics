## Question
What happened to prescription volume and cost when a region made medicines free again for low-income pensioners?

## Method and data
Classical synthetic control and BSTS on monthly prescriptions.

## Findings
- Prescriptions +6.34% (synthetic control) / +6.70% (BSTS, 95% CrI 4.05-9.47).
- Budget cost much larger than the forgone co-payment revenue.

## Limitations
One region; volume and cost only; no adherence or health outcomes.

## Relevance
Best available evidence on what the 2021 national exemption for low-income pensioners likely did (more use, higher cost); health benefit unmeasured.
