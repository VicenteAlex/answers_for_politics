## Question
Did mortality change during the Great Recession in Spain, and differently by socioeconomic group?

## Method and data
Poisson regression of annual mortality reduction, 2004-07 vs 2008-11, by housing floor space and car ownership.

## Findings
- Low group (floor space): annual decline 1.7% (95% CI 1.2-2.1) in 2004-07 vs 3.0% (2.5-3.5) in 2008-11; high group 2.0% vs 2.1%.
- Authors attribute to lower exposure to risk factors during recession.

## Limitations
Observational; ends in 2011, before the 2012 health reforms; not a test of austerity in health care.

## Relevance
Warns against attributing aggregate mortality trends to health policy: the business cycle moves mortality in the opposite direction to what intuition suggests.
