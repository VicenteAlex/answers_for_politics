## Question
How strongly does reported taxable wealth respond to a wealth tax, and through which channels?

## Method and data
Swedish administrative tax data; bunching at the wealth-tax threshold and difference-in-differences designs. Compares self-reported asset categories with categories reported by third parties.

## Findings
- Net-of-tax-rate elasticity of taxable wealth between 0.09 and 0.27.
- About one third of the elasticity comes from under-reporting of asset values.
- Responses reflect evasion and avoidance rather than changes in saving.

## Limitations
Short-run, local responses near the threshold; says little about long-run accumulation or the very top. Card based on the abstract.

## Relevance
Shows modest elasticities near thresholds and that reporting design matters. Applies to self-assessed components of Spain's IP (unlisted shares, valuables, foreign assets).
