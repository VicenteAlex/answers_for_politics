## Question
What were the effects of rent control on rents, rental supply and housing quality, identified from its abrupt removal?

## Method and data
Difference-in-differences around the 1995 statewide referendum that ended rent control in three Massachusetts municipalities, comparing formerly controlled units with comparable uncontrolled units.

## Findings
Not verified against the full text (paywalled; the RePEc record has no abstract). As summarised by later sources:
- Rent caps reduced rents and led to the withdrawal of units from the rental market (Monras and García-Montalvo 2025 VoxEU column).
- Chronic maintenance problems were more frequent in controlled units, and the gap fell after decontrol (as cited in later literature).

## Limitations
Card built from secondary descriptions; effect sizes are not recorded here and must be checked in the article before being quoted. Old and strict US regime.

## Relevance
A classic causal study on supply withdrawal and quality. Its magnitudes should not be transferred directly to Spain's second-generation, index-based controls.
